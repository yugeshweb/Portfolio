Hobbies

Away from the screen.

Travelling
Traveling is more than just a pastime for me; it's a way of life. Exploring new destinations, immersing myself in diverse cultures, and uncovering the beauty of our world bring me immense joy and endless opportunities for personal growth.
Cubing
Solving the Rubik's Cube is my favorite mental escape. The satisfying clicks of each twist and the challenge of aligning every colour sharpen my focus. Whether I'm racing the clock or solving casually, it's a perfect blend of logic, patience, and creativity that keeps my mind engaged.
Gardening
Spending time in the garden is a soothing escape from the routine. Whether it's planting flowers, tending to vegetables, or simply soaking in the green, gardening rewards patience with something that visibly grows.
Photography
Photography is my way of capturing the world's fleeting moments and turning them into lasting memories. With a camera in hand, I explore the beauty of everyday life, seeking to frame the world in a unique and captivating way.
